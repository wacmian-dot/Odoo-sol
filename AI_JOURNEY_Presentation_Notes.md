# How to Deploy the Working-Method Note (YOUR PREP ONLY — the note itself is the handout)

**The artifact:** `AI_Working_Method_Note.pdf` — "The Architect and the Machine", 2 pages, house style, panel-facing.
**It stays OUT of the client zip.** The 15-document package is written to MDR Devices; this note is written to much. Mixing them would break the fiction of the client deliverable. Print it separately (or keep it as the last attachment in your submission email, clearly labeled).

## When to play it
- **Do not open with it.** Lead with the solution and the live demo — competence first, method second.
- **The trigger is the AI question.** When (not if) a panelist asks how you used AI, deliver your existing 30-second line from the elevator pitch ("force multiplier under my direction… the judgment is mine; the leverage is the tool's"), then: *"I actually wrote that up properly — here's the working-method note, including the log of what the tooling got wrong and how I caught it."* Slide it across. Stop talking.
- If the question never comes, hand it over in the close as the final artifact: *"One last page — since you allowed AI, here is exactly how it was used."*

## Why this is memorable (the psychology)
1. **The correction log is the differentiator.** Every candidate will claim responsible AI use; you are the only one who will table a defect register — the hold-date contradiction, the empty consignment location, the mis-sequenced credential gate. Showing what the machine got wrong is the strongest possible proof that a human architect was in charge.
2. **The symmetry lands the hire.** The note's spine — "AI drafts; it never releases, applied to my own toolchain" — is the exact governance rule your proposal sets for MDR Devices' AI layer, and it's much.'s own "dual control principle" vocabulary. You didn't just design governance; you lived under it for a week. One sentence to say out loud: *"The governance I'm proposing for the client's AI is the governance I ran my own tools under — you're looking at the evidence."*
3. **It reframes the AI permission as a test you passed.** They allowed AI partly to see what candidates do with it. The note shows you treated the permission as a professional obligation (verify, audit, peer-review) rather than a shortcut — the "ownership" and "no-nonsense" traits from their own published values.

## Guardrails
- Never name specific AI products or models in the room unless asked directly; "AI tooling / agent runs" is the right altitude. If pressed, answer plainly and return to the governance story.
- Don't oversell the struggle. The correction log is stated matter-of-factly in the note; let it speak. One verbal example maximum (the credential-gate re-sequencing is the best one — it's a *control-logic* catch, pure architect judgment).
- If someone asks "so what did YOU actually do?" — the answer is the note's §3 right-hand column, and the live system: "Every refusal you watched me trigger today was specified by me, built under my direction, and proven by me attempting the non-compliant transaction."
