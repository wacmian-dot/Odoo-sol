# The Opening Two Minutes — Elevator Pitch
*~290 words · ≈2:00 at 140 wpm · say it before touching any document. Structure: Value → Proof → Map → Excitement.*

---

"Before I open a single document, let me tell you what I found and what I did about it.

MDR Devices doesn't have a software problem — they have a coherence problem with regulatory consequences. Four systems that each work fine, governing one continuous clinical event none of them can see: an implant's journey from titanium rod to a patient's hip. Today, 'compliant' at MDR Devices is enforced by memory and tenure — and that white space between systems is a standing MDR Article 27 exposure that compounds with every product they add.

So my proposal is one validated definition of compliant, end to end — I call it the Compliant Digital Thread — built on native Odoo wherever the platform genuinely covers the requirement, with five *named* extensions where it doesn't. We don't sell zero custom code. We sell named custom code.

Now — the case asked us to convey the look and feel of the future solution. I decided the only honest way to do that was to build it. So there's a live Odoo system behind this pitch. Try to confirm an order for a surgeon whose certification lapsed in April — the system refuses. Try to release a lot before its ninety-day hold — refused. Try to dispatch an instrument tray on its fiftieth sterilization cycle — refused. Those aren't slides; I can trigger any of them for you right now.

Where the case was vague, I made grounded assumptions and labelled them — you'll find every one marked 'discovery-scoped' rather than dressed up as fact.

And the part I find genuinely exciting: nobody at MDR Devices has to remember anything anymore. The system carries the compliance burden — the people get to do medicine and business. That's the pitch. Let me show you the thread."
