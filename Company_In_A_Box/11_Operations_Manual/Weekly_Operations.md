<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Once a week, step back: review the KPI dashboard, inspect the pipeline stage-by-stage, and fix the biggest bottleneck. The weekly review is where conversion problems get caught early — before they show up as a revenue miss a month later.</div>

## Weekly agenda

| Item | Question |
|---|---|
| KPI review (Pack 10) | Are leading indicators on target? |
| Pipeline inspection | Where are deals stalling? (which stage gate?) |
| Bottleneck fix | What one change this week moves the most? |
| Trial health | Are active trials on track to convert by day 30? (Pack 12) |
| Forecast update | What's likely to close this month? |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">One bottleneck at a time</span><br>
A lean team can't fix everything at once. The weekly review's discipline is to identify the <b>single</b> biggest constraint (usually a conversion ratio, Pack 10) and improve it, then re-check next week. Focused iteration beats scattered effort.</div>

## Implication

The weekly cadence connects daily activity (this Pack) to monthly outcomes and to the financial model's assumptions (Pack 16) — the loop that keeps the plan grounded in reality.
