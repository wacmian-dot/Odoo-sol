<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Localise a <b>proven</b> Polish installer-CRM (600+ deployments) into the UK's fast-growing, policy-backed renewables market via a lean, founder-led operation — winning the small-installer segment that incumbents Payaca and Reonic price past, funded by a UK Start Up Loan. The opportunity and product are real and verified; two things remain open and gate the rest: the <b>deal structure</b> and <b>UK pricing</b>.</div>

## The opportunity ✅📊
- **Market:** 5,250+ MCS-certified UK installers; solar +31% record in 2025; +50% forecast for 2026; policy-backed (Boiler Upgrade Scheme, 0% solar VAT, 600k heat pumps/yr by 2028 target).
- **Product:** SalesOps CRM (RRUP sp. z o.o.) — proven at scale (15,000+ users, 600+ deployments), end-to-end installer workflow, per-seat pricing.
- **Validation:** Reonic (German, €13M-funded) is entering the same space — independent proof the thesis is investable.

## The strategy
- **Segment:** scaling MCS solar/heat-pump installers, 10–50 staff — underserved by Payaca's £999+/mo flat pricing and Reonic's enterprise tilt.
- **Wedge:** proven platform + per-seat price + cross-vertical breadth + founder-led onboarding.
- **Motion:** free MCS-database outreach → tailored demo → free 30-day trial → conversion.
- **Funding:** UK Start Up Loan (£25–100k) — non-dilutive, fast, with mentoring.

## The honest open items ❓
<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Two gates + one flag</span><br>
<b>(1) Deal structure</b> — equity JV vs commission/agency — unresolved and gates legal/funding/economics (Pack 14, Q1). <b>(2) UK pricing</b> — unset, gates the model (Pack 16, Q). <b>Flag:</b> a prior "Polsat" flagship-client claim is <b>unverified</b> and must not be used until the CEO confirms it (Pack 02, Q6).</div>

## The verdict ⚠️
A **defensible, fundable, capital-efficient venture** — not speculative. The product is proven, the market real and growing, the entry lean and low-risk. Success depends on execution against a focused segment and on resolving the two open items with the CEO. The package that follows maps every aspect end-to-end and is built to update the moment those answers arrive.
