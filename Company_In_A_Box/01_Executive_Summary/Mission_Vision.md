<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
<b>Mission:</b> give growing UK renewables and trade installers one proven, affordable system to run their whole business — so they can scale without drowning in admin. <b>Vision:</b> become the default operating platform for the UK's small-and-mid installer market as it triples toward the 2028 heat-pump targets.</div>

## Mission
Help growing UK installers replace spreadsheets, WhatsApp and manual paperwork with one proven, per-seat-priced platform that runs lead-to-cash — freeing them to grow rather than firefight.

## Vision
As the UK races toward 600,000 heat pumps a year by 2028, become the go-to operating system for the small-and-mid installer segment the big platforms overlook — built on a proven engine, delivered with genuine local care.

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Why this framing works</span><br>
The mission is customer-outcome-led (help installers scale), not product-led (sell CRM) — which keeps the whole operation pointed at the thing that actually drives referrals and retention in a trust-based trade. The vision is ambitious but bounded to a real, reachable segment (Pack 03), matching the honest, capital-efficient posture of the whole plan.</div>

## Implication
Mission and vision anchor the positioning (Pack 04) and the growth story (Pack 05/18): every decision serves helping installers grow, in the segment the venture can genuinely win.
