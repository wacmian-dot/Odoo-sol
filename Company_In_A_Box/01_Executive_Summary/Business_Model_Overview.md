<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Recurring per-seat SaaS revenue from UK installers, sold through a lean, low-cost motion (free lead channel + free trial), delivered on a proven platform with founder-led onboarding, with revenue shared with RRUP per the (open) deal structure. Capital-light in, recurring revenue out.</div>

## The model on one page

| Element | This venture |
|---|---|
| **Create value** | One proven system replacing installers' fragmented tools |
| **Deliver value** | Per-seat subscription + hands-on 30-day onboarding |
| **Capture value** | Recurring monthly revenue; per-seat expansion as customers grow |
| **Cost to serve** | Lean pod; free primary lead channel; gated spend |
| **Share with RRUP** | Per deal structure (equity split or commission — ❓ Pack 14) |
| **Fund it** | UK Start Up Loan; recurring revenue services repayment |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Why the model is attractive</span><br>
It combines <b>low entry cost</b> (proven product, free lead channel, loan funding) with <b>compounding recurring revenue</b> (per-seat SaaS that grows with retention and account expansion). That's a capital-efficient, resilient shape — the kind that survives a slow start and compounds a good one.</div>

## Implication
The model is deliberately capital-light and recurring — matching the lean funding (Pack 17) and the bounded-but-real market (Pack 03). The one variable that most changes its economics is the revenue share with RRUP (Pack 14/16).
