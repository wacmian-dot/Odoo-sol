<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
This is the highest-value technical verification: <b>what UK-specific functionality already exists</b> (MCS/DNO/ECO4 fields, UK VAT, GBP invoicing, MCS-compliant proposal templates) versus what must be built. It determines what you can promise a prospect and how fast you can onboard — and it's currently unknown because the demo was down.</div>

## Localisation questions (verify in a live demo)

1. Are the MCS / ECO4 / DNO "procedure patterns" pre-built configuration templates, or built from scratch per client? *(This directly affects onboarding speed and cost — Pack 12.)*
2. Does the platform handle UK VAT (including 0% on qualifying energy-saving materials) and GBP invoicing natively?
3. How modular is the CPQ/pricing logic for UK technical metrics — BTU, heat-pump COP, roof-pitch/shading for solar?
4. Are there UK-compliant, branded proposal/contract templates ready to use?

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Assume build-from-scratch until proven otherwise</span><br>
Until verified in a working demo, the plan conservatively assumes UK localisation must be built (flagged throughout the package). If RRUP has already done much of it (Pack 22, Q7), that's upside — faster launch, lower cost. Either way, <b>never promise a UK compliance feature to a prospect that you haven't seen working</b> (Pack 09).</div>

## Implication

This verification unblocks honest, confident selling (Pack 09) and accurate cost/timeline (Pack 16). It's the top item for the first working-demo session with RRUP — arguably the single most important technical task in the whole package.
