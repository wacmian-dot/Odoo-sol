<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Follow the demo structure (Pack 09): recap their pain, show their workflow solved, land the two differentiators, close on the free trial. Tailor to the prospect's trade. Never show or promise a feature you haven't demo-verified.</div>

## Procedure

1. **Prep** — confirm the demo environment is live that morning (it was down once — never skip this). Pre-configure to the prospect's trade/branding where possible.
2. **Recap pain** (0–3 min) — "You told me X takes too long and Y eats hours…"
3. **Show their workflow** (3–10) — lead → quote → e-signed contract → job → auto commission.
4. **Two differentiators** (10–15) — Work-Flow automation; one system across their trades.
5. **Proof + price** (15–18) — 600+ deployments; per-seat, start small.
6. **Trial close** (18–20) — "Let's get your data in and your team live free for 30 days."
7. **Log** — outcome + next step in CRM; if trial agreed, trigger onboarding (Pack 12).

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Hard rules</span><br>
Confirm the environment is live before every demo. Never reference Polsat. Never claim UK-compliance features not demo-verified (Pack 20). Never quote a fixed UK price before RRUP sets it.</div>

## Implication

A disciplined, tailored demo is the pod's highest-conversion asset. Pre-built vertical demo environments (Pack 09) make this SOP fast to run well.
