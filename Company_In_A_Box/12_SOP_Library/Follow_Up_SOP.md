<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Most prospects aren't ready today but will be. A light, automated nurture keeps you top-of-mind so that when their pain peaks (or their incumbent contract ends), you're the call they make. Persistence, not pressure.</div>

## Procedure

1. **Tag the reason** a prospect isn't advancing (too small, mid-contract, no budget now).
2. **Set a re-touch date** (e.g. 60–90 days; or the incumbent's renewal date if known).
3. **Automated value touches** in between — a case study, a useful compliance update (Pack 07) — not repeated "just checking in."
4. **Re-engage** on the date with a fresh, specific reason to talk.
5. **Log** every touch and response.

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Nurture is compounding pipeline</span><br>
A disciplined nurture list becomes a compounding asset: every month it grows, and a steady trickle matures into live deals. For a lean team, it's free future pipeline — as long as it's actually worked, not just stored.</div>

## Implication

Nurture protects the effort already spent on qualified prospects and smooths future pipeline — a quiet but real contributor to the funnel (Pack 08).
