<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Run discovery to the MEDDIC-informed spine (Pack 09): surface 2–3 quantified pains, identify the economic buyer, and confirm ICP-fit before committing a demo. A deal without quantified pain is not ready to advance.</div>

## Procedure

1. **Open** — confirm you're speaking to / can reach the decision-maker (Pack 06 personas).
2. **Diagnose** — ask the pain questions: quote turnaround, deals slipped, admin/compliance hours, commission errors (Pack 09).
3. **Quantify** — turn each pain into the prospect's own £ or hours figure (never invent).
4. **Qualify** — ICP-fit? economic buyer engaged? decision process/timeline understood?
5. **Advance or nurture** — if 2+ pains quantified and buyer engaged, book the tailored demo; else nurture.
6. **Log** — record pains, figures, buyer, next step in CRM (Stage 3).

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The gate</span><br>
Do not book a demo without quantified pain. A demo into a vague "just looking" wastes the lean team's scarcest resource. Qualify hard; demo only the ready.</div>

## Implication

Good discovery is the highest-leverage skill in the motion (Pack 09) — it's what lets a small team win more from fewer demos.
