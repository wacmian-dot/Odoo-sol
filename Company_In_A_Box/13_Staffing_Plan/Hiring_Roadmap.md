<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Hire in trigger order, not calendar order: <b>first the role that most constrains growth</b> (usually the Implementation lead or SDR), only once the founder is at capacity and revenue supports it. No hire is made on hope; each is earned by proof.</div>

## Roadmap (trigger-gated ⚠️)

| Order | Hire | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Implementation lead OR SDR | Founder can't cover onboarding AND outreach; ~3+ trials in flight or pipeline demands more outreach |
| 2 | The other of the two | First hire freed founder but the other function now constrains |
| 3 | Vertical AE (if founder was closing) | Deal volume exceeds founder's close capacity |
| 4+ | Regional expansion team | Positive unit economics; Tier-2/3 push (Pack 06) |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Which role first?</span><br>
Usually the <b>Implementation lead</b> — because trial→paid conversion (Pack 16's top lever) suffers most when the founder is too stretched to onboard well, and a lost trial is a lost customer. But if the constraint is clearly top-of-funnel volume, the SDR comes first. Let the bottleneck (Pack 11 weekly review) decide.</div>

## Implication

This roadmap keeps the cost base matched to traction (Pack 16) and is exactly the discipline a Start Up Loan mentor (Pack 17) and the CEO will want to see.
