<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Three roles cover the whole motion. Hire the <b>SDR</b> for outreach volume, the <b>AE</b> for conversion, and the <b>Implementation lead</b> for retention. Compensation benchmarks below are 2026 UK market data — use them to budget, not as offers before benchmarking the specific candidate.</div>

## The three roles

| Role | Does | Source from | Measured on | Comp (2026 UK 📊) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Outbound SDR** | Prospecting, cadence, booking demos (Pack 08) | Trade/merchant sales or outbound SaaS backgrounds | Qualified demos booked | Base £25–40k; OTE £35–60k |
| **Vertical AE** | Discovery→demo→close (Pack 09) | UK renewables/HVAC/trade-software sales | New recurring revenue | Base £47–57k; OTE £70–110k+ |
| **Implementation lead** | 30-day onboarding, retention (Pack 12) | Field-service/implementation backgrounds | Trial→paid, retention | Benchmark against ops/CS roles |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Hire for the motion, not for titles</span><br>
The AE must be able to run a technical vertical demo, not just talk; the Implementation lead is a retention asset, not admin. These roles are the difference between the funnel math working and not (Pack 08/16) — hire deliberately, and only when triggered (Org_Chart).</div>

## Implication

These descriptions are ready to post the moment the hiring trigger is hit (Org_Chart). Comp figures anchor the cost side of the financial model (Pack 16 Assumptions Register).
