<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Fill the pipeline in a deliberate order: <b>free, targeted MCS-database outreach from day one</b>; trade shows for reach and credibility on the fixed 2026 calendar; RECC/HIES channel partnerships once there are UK references. Lead with the cheapest, most-qualified source; layer paid/partnership channels as the business earns them.</div>

## 1. Channel priority (cost × speed × fit)

| Priority | Channel | Cost | Speed | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MCS-database direct outreach | ~Free | Immediate | Perfect (every target is ICP) |
| 2 | Trade shows (calendar below) | Med–High | Event-timed | High |
| 3 | RECC / HIES partnership | Low | Slow (relationship) | High + credibility |
| 4 | Content / SEO (Pack 07) | Low–Med | Slow build | Medium |
| 5 | Paid ads / bought data | Med | Fast | Medium (validate CAC) |
| 6 | RRUP warm referrals (EU customers with UK links) | ~Free | Depends on RRUP | High (❓ ask RRUP) |

## 2. The fixed 2026 trade-show calendar ✅

| Event | Dates | Scale | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Futurebuild | 12–14 May 2026, ExCeL London | 26,590 visitors | Construction-vertical reach |
| InstallerSHOW | 23–26 Jun 2026, NEC Birmingham | 40,000+ visitors | Largest installer audience |
| Solar & Storage Live | 22–24 Sep 2026, NEC Birmingham | 20,000+ pros, "Meet the Installers" zone | Most ICP-dense |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Sequencing insight</span><br>
Don't wait for a trade show to start selling. The MCS-database motion generates qualified pipeline in <b>week one</b> at near-zero cost. Trade shows then amplify reach and warm the market — but they're an accelerant on an already-running engine, not the ignition.</div>

## 3. Month-one focus

The SDR builds the target list (Pack 06) and runs the outreach sequences (see Outreach_Sequences) against the beachhead region. Everything else layers on top once pipeline is moving.

## Implication

Because the primary channel is free and immediately available, the venture is not gated on marketing budget to start generating pipeline — which materially de-risks the early funding position (Pack 16/17).
