## 1. The lean launch pod ✅ (structure) / ⚠️ (comp numbers — illustrative, not costed)

A 3-role team under you, rather than hiring ahead of revenue:

| Role | Core function | Weekly metric |
|---|---|---|
| **Outbound SDR** | ABM + cold outreach targeting Tier-2 contractors (£5–50M revenue) across North West/Midlands/Yorkshire | ~40–60 outreaches/day |
| **Vertical Account Executive** | Runs vertical-specific demos, CPQ/pricing walkthroughs, contract close via e-signature | 10–15 demos/week |
| **Technical Implementation Consultant** | Owns the 30-day onboarding window — legacy data migration, compliance config, field-crew training. Protects retention, not sales-comped. | 80%+ trial-to-paid conversion |

Sourcing: SDR from UK trade-merchant/outbound backgrounds, AE from UK renewables/HVAC/trade-software sales, Technical Consultant from field-service-management implementation backgrounds. **The specific comp numbers (base+bonus tiers, equity pool %) are illustrative placeholders — benchmark UK SaaS SDR/AE comp before using them in an actual job offer, and don't hire ahead of confirming the deal structure in Folder 13.**

## 2. The 30-day onboarding framework ✅

Matches RRUP's own 30-day free trial exactly, and directly targets the well-documented pattern that most CRM churn happens in the first 30 days from poor data migration and low adoption:

- **Days 1–7:** legacy data ingestion — pull the prospect off spreadsheets/WhatsApp/whatever they're using now, migrate historic customer records.
- **Days 8–21:** configure the client's specific pricing catalogs, CPQ rules, and UK compliance workflows (MCS/DNO/ECO4 fields) into the system.
- **Days 22–30:** live field-crew and office-admin training, verify mobile/photo sync, confirm adoption before the trial ends.

## 3. 90-day phased setup roadmap ✅

- **Days 1–30:** legal entity + co-working office (Manchester — Spinningfields/Ancoats are sensible low-capex options), GBP/VAT configuration, UK regulatory template ingestion (MCS/ECO4/DNO fields).
- **Days 31–60:** hire the pod, two-week onboarding, launch outreach across North West/Midlands/Yorkshire, first wave of trial sign-ups.
- **Days 61–90:** activate the onboarding framework for all live trials, integrate with Xero/Sage/QuickBooks (the three that dominate UK SME cloud accounting).

**This roadmap assumes hiring ahead of revenue is affordable** — revisit the pacing once Folder 08's funding picture and Folder 13's deal-structure answer are both confirmed. A commission/agency structure might mean you run solo for longer before the pod makes sense; an equity JV with committed capital from both sides might support hiring sooner.

## 4. Manchester as the hub ✅

Independent of anything unverified elsewhere in this package: Greater Manchester's 2038 carbon-neutral target (12 years ahead of the national 2050 date) is real and confirmed directly by the Greater Manchester Combined Authority, and it correlates with genuine trade-contractor density in the North West. Worth keeping as the launch city regardless of how the deal-structure question resolves.
