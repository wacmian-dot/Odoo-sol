## 1. Why this folder is a framework, not a model yet ⚠️

A real unit-economics model needs inputs this package doesn't have yet: confirmed UK pricing, confirmed deal structure (equity share of revenue vs. commission %), and real customer acquisition cost data (which only exists once outreach starts). What follows is the **shape** of the model to build, with placeholders clearly marked — filling it in properly is a near-term next step, not something to fabricate now.

## 4. What a completed model would answer

Once the inputs above are real: how many UK customers are needed to cover the launch pod's costs (breakeven customer count), how long the runway from a Start Up Loan or personal capital actually lasts at a realistic sales velocity, and what CAC threshold makes each acquisition channel (cold outreach vs. trade shows vs. paid data) worth the spend.

## 5. Recommended next step

Once Folder 13 is answered and UK pricing is set, this is the single highest-value document to properly build out — everything else in this package supports the go-to-market, but this is what tells you whether the go-to-market plan actually pays for itself on a realistic timeline.
