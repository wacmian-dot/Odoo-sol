## Context: the reset
The prior sales partner ("Bart") misrepresented facts; the CEO identified this and asked to deal directly. This is a clean restart — these questions establish a verified shared baseline before any further commitment.

## The ten questions

**1. Deal structure — equity or agency?** Is this an equity joint venture (I co-own/operate a UK company) or a commission/agency arrangement (I'm paid a % of contract value, no equity)? Everything else depends on this.

**2. What did Bart actually represent to you, on my behalf?** I want to reconcile what he told you was agreed against what I understood, so we start from a confirmed baseline rather than carry forward anything he misrepresented.

**3. What does each side contribute?** Product/IP licence, the SalesOps CRM brand and site, capital — versus what you expect me to fund or build.

**4. Is there any existing UK presence, pipeline or leads?** The website makes UK-market claims — is there anything real behind them, or am I starting from zero?

**5. Can you share real, checkable reference clients?** Your largest/most notable clients, in enough detail that I can responsibly reference them in UK pitches.

**6. Is Grupa Polsat Plus a genuine client?** It appeared in earlier material as a flagship reference; I couldn't verify it and want to hear it from you directly before it's ever used.

**7. What UK localisation already exists?** VAT/GBP handling, MCS/DNO/ECO4 fields, UK proposal templates — built, or on me to scope and build?

**8. Would I have exclusive UK territory, and on what terms?** And what would change or revoke it?

**9. How do the commercial mechanics work?** Commission on TCV or recurring, renewals included? Or if equity: what %, and vesting/forfeiture triggers?

**10. What timeline and review point are you working to?** What does success look like in 6–12 months, and what happens if it takes longer?

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Why this order</span><br>
Questions 1 and 6 are first because they gate the most: the deal structure determines the legal form, funding and your risk; the Polsat answer determines whether a central credibility asset is real. Resolve these two and most of the package's open flags close.</div>
