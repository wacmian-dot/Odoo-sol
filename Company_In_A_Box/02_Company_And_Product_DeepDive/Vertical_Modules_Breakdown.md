## 2. Why breadth is the wedge

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The multi-trade trend</span><br>
UK renewables installers increasingly bundle solar, heat pumps and battery storage for the same homeowner (the "combo" pattern recurs across UK market coverage). An installer doing all three either runs three single-vertical tools or one platform that spans them. SalesOps CRM's breadth means it can be the one platform — a structural advantage that single-vertical competitors cannot easily match.</div>

## 3. First-focus recommendation ⚠️

Do not lead the UK entry with all eight verticals. Lead with **Solar PV and Heat Pumps** — the two with the largest, fastest-growing, most directly-reachable UK installer base (Pack 03, Pack 06) — and hold the breadth as the expansion story once the beachhead is won. Trying to be everything to every trade on day one dilutes the pitch.
