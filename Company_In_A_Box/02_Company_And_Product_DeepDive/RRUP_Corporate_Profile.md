## 3. The two-brand structure ❗

RRUP operates its Polish-market product as **RRUP CRM** and its UK-facing product as **SalesOps CRM** — the same underlying engine, a different brand for a different market. Public search descriptions already position SalesOps CRM in superlative UK terms ("the most extensive CRM for the HRV systems industry available on the UK market").

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The gap to close</span><br>
The UK <b>brand and marketing claims already exist</b>, but — per the situation described — there is no UK team, customer base, or support operation behind them yet. The website is ahead of the business. Making those published claims <b>true</b> is a large part of the job this partnership exists to do. This is a strength to name openly with the CEO, not a problem to hide.</div>

The corporate foundation is sound.
