<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The 90-day arc: <b>Days 1–30 set up and start outreach</b>, <b>Days 31–60 run demos and start trials</b>, <b>Days 61–90 convert the first trials and secure the first UK references</b>. By day 90 the goal is first paying customers and a validated (or corrected) funnel.</div>

## The three phases

| Phase | Days | Focus | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Setup & outreach** | 1–30 | Gates in progress; CRM live; target list built; outreach begins (Pack 21 30-day) | Pipeline started |
| **Demo & trial** | 31–60 | Run demos (Pack 09); start free trials; onboard (Pack 12) | Trials active |
| **Convert & reference** | 61–90 | Drive trial→paid; secure first case studies (Pack 07); integrate accounting (Pack 20) | First customers + references |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The 90-day prize</span><br>
The single most valuable Day-90 outcome is <b>2–3 paying UK customers willing to be references</b>. Those references unlock everything downstream — easier next sales, trade-show credibility, RECC/HIES partnerships. Prioritise reference-quality early wins over raw volume.</div>

## Implication
Ninety days should validate (or correct) the funnel assumptions (Pack 08/16) with real data, and produce the first references. That evidence gates the move into expansion (Pack 05).
