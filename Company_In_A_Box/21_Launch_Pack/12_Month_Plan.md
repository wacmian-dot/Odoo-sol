<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Year one is the <b>prove</b> phase (Pack 05): win a beachhead cluster, validate the motion, keep the base lean, and reach ~30 customers (base case). The year ends with the evidence needed to justify expansion and first hires — traction, not calendar, gates the pace.</div>

## The 12-month arc

| Quarter | Focus | Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Setup, first customers, references (Pack 21 90-day) | 2–3 referenceable customers |
| Q2 | Repeatable motion; first trade show (InstallerSHOW, Jun) | Validated funnel; growing pipeline |
| Q3 | Scale outreach; Solar & Storage Live (Sep); first hire if triggered (Pack 13) | Pod forming; steady acquisition |
| Q4 | Consolidate; document playbook; plan expansion (Pack 06 Tier 2) | ~30 customers; expansion case ready |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The year's real deliverable</span><br>
The tangible Year-1 output isn't just revenue — it's a <b>documented, repeatable playbook</b> plus references plus validated unit economics. That package is what unlocks Year-2 expansion (and any growth funding, Pack 18). Prove it works, write down how, then scale it.</div>

## Implication
The 12-month plan aligns with the financial Year-1 forecast (Pack 16) and the growth gates (Pack 05). Its pace flexes with the deal structure and funding (both partly CEO-gated).
