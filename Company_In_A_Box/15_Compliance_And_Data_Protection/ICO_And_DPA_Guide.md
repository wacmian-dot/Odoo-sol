<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Two actions clear most of the compliance obligation: <b>(1) register with the ICO</b> (quick, low-cost, online), and <b>(2) put a Data Processing Agreement in place with RRUP</b> (drafted with the partnership agreement). Do both at formation and the operation starts lawful.</div>

## Action 1 — ICO registration

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | The UK entity (once formed, Pack 14) |
| How | Online via the ICO; pay the data-protection fee |
| When | At formation — before processing personal data at scale |
| Output | Registered controller; on the public register |

## Action 2 — Data Processing Agreement with RRUP

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Why | Personal data flows to RRUP's EU servers; roles must be defined |
| What | Controller/processor terms; cross-border transfer mechanism; security obligations |
| With whom | RRUP (Pack 14); drafted by the solicitor (Pack 14 Solicitor_Brief) |
| When | Alongside the partnership agreement |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Fold into the legal engagement</span><br>
Both actions ride along with the company formation and partnership drafting you're already doing (Pack 14). Handling them together is efficient and ensures nothing is missed — put them explicitly on the launch checklist (Pack 21).</div>

## Implication

Two concrete, low-cost actions convert an abstract obligation into a closed item. This is compliance done properly and cheaply, at the right time.
