<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The main repayment risks — slow adoption, deal-structure delay, competition — are the same ones the whole plan already mitigates through a lean base and gated spend. Because the cost base is low and the lead channel is free, the venture can service a modest loan even if growth disappoints.</div>

## Risk → mitigation (repayment lens)

| Risk to repayment | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Slow customer acquisition | Lean base means low monthly burn; loan term is long; free MCS channel keeps trying at near-zero cost |
| Deal structure unresolved delays launch | Loan pursued in parallel; founder can begin market-building (outreach) before final signing |
| Competition compresses growth | Focused segment + price wedge; not competing on spend |
| Personal circumstances | Unsecured personal loan — understood; mitigated by conservative amount and long term |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The core protection</span><br>
Low fixed costs + recurring revenue + a long, flexible repayment term = a loan that can be serviced across a wide range of outcomes. The plan does not need everything to go right to repay — that asymmetry is the strongest repayment-risk mitigation there is.</div>

## Implication

The loan is a sensible, well-protected first capital source. Combined with the free mentoring it carries, it both funds and strengthens the venture.
