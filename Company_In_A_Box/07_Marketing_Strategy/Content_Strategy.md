<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Build a <b>small library of proof and practical value</b>, not a blog treadmill: UK installer case studies, a compliance-workflow explainer, an ROI/"cost of the leaky bucket" calculator, and demo videos. Each asset directly supports a sales conversation.</div>

## The core assets

| Asset | Job |
|---|---|
| UK installer case studies | The single most persuasive asset — build as first UK customers land |
| "Cost of the status quo" calculator | Turns the leaky-bucket pitch (Pack 08/09) into a self-serve hook |
| MCS/compliance workflow explainer | Demonstrates domain fluency to a compliance-anxious buyer |
| Vertical demo videos | Let prospects self-qualify before a live demo |

## The principle

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Proof over volume</span><br>
In a trust-driven trade, one credible UK peer case study outperforms fifty generic blog posts. Content effort concentrates on <b>proof and practical tools</b> that a salesperson can send mid-deal, not on SEO volume for its own sake.</div>

## Boundary

Case studies must be real and permissioned — never fabricated (Pack 02 discipline). Until UK references exist, lean on the verified platform proof points and Polish references, honestly framed.

## Implication

Content is a sales-enablement function here, not a standalone demand channel. It compounds as UK references accumulate (Pack 05 flywheel).
