<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Two compounding, low-cost engines: <b>customer referrals</b> (a happy installer's word carries enormous weight in a tight-knit trade) and <b>trade-body partnerships</b> (RECC/HIES reach the entire ICP with borrowed credibility). Both get more powerful as UK references accumulate.</div>

## 1. Referral engine

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Word-of-mouth is the trade's real marketing</span><br>
Installers talk — at merchants, at trade shows, in WhatsApp groups. A structured referral incentive (e.g. account credit for a successful referral) turns satisfied early customers into a low-CAC acquisition channel. This is the highest-trust, lowest-cost channel available and it compounds with the reference flywheel (Pack 05).</div>

## 2. Trade-body partnerships ✅

| Body | Opportunity |
|---|---|
| RECC (Renewable Energy Consumer Code) | Partner directory listing, newsletter, joint webinar — reaches MCS installers |
| HIES | Similar reach across a broader home-energy remit |

Every MCS installer must join an approved consumer code, so RECC/HIES are a direct line to the whole ICP. Partnership terms are to be agreed (⚠️), and become far more attractive once you have UK references to point to.

## 3. RRUP referrals ❓

RRUP's existing EU customers with UK operations, or EU installers themselves expanding to the UK, are a warm channel RRUP can open — worth asking directly (Pack 22).

## Implication

Referral and channel partnerships are the mature-phase CAC reducers. They can't carry the launch (no references yet), but building the referral mechanics early and opening RECC/HIES conversations after the first wins sets up a durable, low-cost growth engine.
