<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
For larger installers already running SAP, Xero, Sage or QuickBooks, do <b>not</b> position SalesOps CRM as a replacement. Position it as an agile <b>system of engagement</b> that sits on top of their existing <b>system of record</b> via API — handling the trade-specific work their ERP does badly, without threatening the finance backbone they will never rip out.</div>

## 1. The thesis

Large firms keep their general ledger and accounting in a heavy system of record (SAP/Xero/Sage) for good reasons — regulatory reporting, sunk cost, finance-team familiarity. Trying to displace that is a losing, multi-month fight. But those systems are poor at trade-specific field work: solar array layout, heat-pump flow/COP sizing, on-site quoting, crew dispatch, compliance capture.

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The winning frame</span><br>
"Keep your finance system exactly as it is. We sit on top of it as the fast front-end your field and sales teams actually use, and we pipe the clean data back into it via the API. You get the trade-specific workflow without a risky finance migration — and without the tens of thousands it would cost to bolt this onto your ERP."</div>

## 2. Why it works

- **Removes the biggest objection** ("we're not replacing SAP") before it's raised.
- **Reframes the purchase** from "rip and replace" (scary, slow) to "add a productivity layer" (safe, fast).
- **Leans on a verified strength** — the open API (Pack 02) — not an unverified claim.

## 3. Boundaries

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Scope honestly</span><br>
The specific integration depth to any given ERP must be scoped with RRUP's technical team (Pack 20) — do not promise a turnkey SAP integration you haven't confirmed. Position the <i>architecture and intent</i> (which is sound and standard), and scope the specific build in discovery.</div>

## 4. Implication

This is the Tier-3 expansion play (Pack 06) — hold it until the beachhead is won and the API integration path is confirmed with RRUP. Used at the right time, it opens larger deals that price-led positioning alone cannot.
