<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Every likely objection has an honest, verified answer already in this package. The rule: <b>never bluff</b>. A flagged "we'll confirm that in your trial" beats a confident wrong claim — it is also, for a newcomer, more credible.</div>

## Objection → response matrix

| Objection | Response (verified) |
|---|---|
| "You're new to the UK." | "The company isn't new — 600+ live deployments and 15,000+ users. What's new is our UK operation, which means you get founder-level attention. And you can prove it yourself in a free 30-day trial." |
| "Why not Payaca?" | "Payaca's excellent — and £999+/month flat plus £1,500 to onboard. We're priced per seat, so you start at a fraction of that and scale as you grow. Try both." |
| "Will it handle UK compliance (MCS/DNO/ECO4)?" | "Let's confirm exactly that in your trial with your real jobs — I'll show you rather than tell you." (Do not over-claim; Pack 20.) |
| "Switching is too disruptive." | "That's why a dedicated implementation lead migrates your data and gets your team live inside the first week of the free trial — no big-bang switch." |
| "Can I see a UK reference?" | "We're building our first UK references now — you could be an early one on preferential terms. Meanwhile here are our proven deployment numbers and Polish references." |

## The one rule

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Never do this</span><br>
Do not invoke the Polsat claim to answer the credibility objection (Pack 02) — it is unverified. Do not assert UK compliance features you haven't demo-verified. Do not quote a fixed UK price before RRUP sets it. The proven-scale + free-trial combination answers the credibility question honestly and well.</div>

## Implication

This matrix is refined continuously by the boardroom red-team simulation (Pack 5A / _SIMULATION) and by real objections from the field (Pack 10). It is a living document — update it as new objections surface.
