## What this package is

This is a complete operational reference for evaluating and building the SalesOps CRM UK expansion — RRUP sp. z o.o.'s (Białystok, Poland) planned entry into the UK market, with you as the on-the-ground partner. Every subfolder covers one aspect of the business end to end, with the evidence and reasoning behind it, not just conclusions.

**Read this folder first.** It tells you what's verified, what's still open, and in what order to use the rest of the package.

## Status of the information in this package — read before acting on any of it

This package is built to the same discipline throughout: every claim is marked **✅ Verified** (from a primary source I checked directly), **📊 Public data**, **⚠️ Framework/estimate** (needs your input to firm up), or **❓ Open question**. A small number of items carry **🚩 Audit finding** — claims from earlier planning material that did not survive independent verification.

**Two things are unresolved and gate almost everything else in this package:**

1. **The deal structure.** Earlier planning material described two incompatible arrangements — an equity joint venture (you as co-owner of a UK operating company) versus a commission/agency arrangement (you as a paid sales agent with no equity). These have different legal, tax, and funding consequences. This is Question 1 in Folder 13.
2. **An unverified enterprise-client claim.** Earlier material asserted that Grupa Polsat Plus, a major Polish conglomerate, is RRUP's flagship client. I could not independently verify this anywhere, including on RRUP's own website. **Do not repeat this claim to anyone — RRUP, a prospect, a lender — until RRUP confirms it directly.** This is Question 6 in Folder 13.

Everything downstream in this package (funding options in Folder 08, legal structure in Folder 07, the pitch playbook in Folder 06) is written to be genuinely useful regardless of how those two questions resolve, but will sharpen significantly once they're answered.

## Recommended order of use

1. Send the ten questions in Folder 13 to the CEO.
2. While waiting for answers, read Folders 01–06 (company, market, competitors, customer, leads, pitch) — none of this depends on the CEO's answers.
3. Once the CEO responds, revisit Folders 07–10 (legal, funding, operations, financials) — these depend directly on knowing which deal structure is real.
4. Folder 12's technical questions go to RRUP's engineering team once you have a technical point of contact — likely after the commercial terms in Folder 13 are settled.

## What this package deliberately does not contain

No fabricated revenue projections, no drafted legal agreements, no invented client names or case studies, no claims about SalesOps CRM's UK product beyond what's independently verifiable. Where real information is missing, the relevant document says so explicitly and states what would resolve it — consistent throughout every document in this package.
