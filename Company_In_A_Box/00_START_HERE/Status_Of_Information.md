<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
This package separates what's <b>verified</b> from what's <b>estimated</b> from what's <b>open</b>, on every claim. The market, product and competitor facts are verified from primary sources. The financials are illustrative frameworks pending CEO inputs. Two items are explicitly flagged as unresolved. Nothing is dressed up as more certain than it is.</div>

## The flag system (used throughout)

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ Verified | Confirmed from a primary source (cited in the doc's footer) |
| 📊 Public data | From public research; reliable but third-party |
| ⚠️ Assumption/estimate | A working figure to validate — not a fact |
| ❓ Open — CEO | Awaiting the CEO's answer (Pack 22) |
| 🚩 Audit flag | A prior claim that failed verification |

## What sits in each bucket

| Verified ✅/📊 | Illustrative ⚠️ | Open ❓/🚩 |
|---|---|---|
| RRUP scale, product, pricing | Financial forecasts | Deal structure (Q1) |
| UK market data | Conversion/CAC/churn | UK pricing |
| Competitor pricing/funding | Customer targets | Polsat client claim (🚩) |
| Trade shows, funding terms | Comp/cost estimates | UK localisation depth |

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">The one rule for the reader</span><br>
Before acting on any number, check its flag. A ✅ figure you can rely on; a ⚠️ figure is a scenario to validate; a ❓ item needs the CEO. This honesty is deliberate — it's the difference between a plan you can trust and one that misleads (the exact failure this whole reset exists to avoid).</div>
