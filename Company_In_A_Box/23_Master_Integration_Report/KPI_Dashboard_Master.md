<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Track a balanced scorecard across four lenses — <b>pipeline, conversion, finance, partnership</b>. Leading indicators (activity, conversion) predict; lagging ones (revenue, retention) confirm; the partnership lens keeps the RRUP relationship healthy. Watch conversion and cash most closely.</div>

## The master scorecard

| Lens | KPI | Illustrative target (⚠️) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline** | Demos held / week | 10–15 | Pack 10 |
| **Pipeline** | Trials started / month | ~5–12 | Pack 08 |
| **Conversion** | Trial → paid | 40%+ | Pack 16 (key lever) |
| **Finance** | New customers / month | 3 (base) | Pack 16 |
| **Finance** | MRR (your share) | Growing | Pack 16 |
| **Finance** | Cash runway (months) | Positive vs loan | Pack 16 |
| **Retention** | Churn | <15% | Pack 16 |
| **Partnership** | Open terms with RRUP | Zero unresolved | Pack 14 |
| **Partnership** | UK references secured | 2–3 by Day 90 | Pack 21 |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The two to watch above all</span><br>
<b>Trial→paid conversion</b> (the model's dominant lever) and <b>cash runway</b> (what actually keeps the venture alive). If those two are healthy, the venture is healthy. Everything else is diagnostic detail.</div>

## Implication
This scorecard is the monthly operating review (Pack 11) and the early-warning system for the financial model (Pack 16). Targets firm up as pilot data replaces assumptions.
