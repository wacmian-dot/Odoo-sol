<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
One timeline: <b>Weeks 1–4</b> close the gates and start the engine; <b>Months 1–3</b> reach first customers; <b>Months 4–12</b> prove and document the motion; <b>Year 2–3</b> expand and scale. Each phase is gated on proof, not calendar.</div>

## Integrated roadmap

| Horizon | Milestones | Packs |
|---|---|---|
| **Weeks 1–4** | CEO questions sent; solicitor engaged; loan applied; CRM + list built; outreach begun | 22, 14, 17, 10, 06, 08 |
| **Months 1–3** | Deal documented; demos + trials running; 2–3 first customers/references | 09, 12, 07 |
| **Months 4–12** | Repeatable motion; trade shows; first hire (gated); ~30 customers; playbook documented | 05, 08, 13, 21 |
| **Year 2** | Expand adjacent segments/regions; pod formed; ~90–120 customers | 06, 16 |
| **Year 3** | Self-funding; Tier-3 SAP deals; ~200–300 customers | 09, 16 |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Gated, not fixed</span><br>
The horizons are a plan, not a promise. Each phase advances only when its evidence gate is met (Pack 05). This protects a loan-funded venture from spending ahead of proof — and is exactly the discipline a lender and a partner-CEO want to see.</div>

## Implication
This roadmap integrates the launch (Pack 21), growth (Pack 05) and financial (Pack 16) timelines into one view. Pace depends on the CEO's answers and funding.
