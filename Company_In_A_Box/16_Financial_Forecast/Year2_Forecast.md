## Dependency

Year 2 is explicitly gated on Year-1 proof (repeatable motion, validated conversion). If Year 1 disappoints, Year 2 stays lean rather than expanding into unproven demand.

## Implication

Year 2 is the year the venture demonstrates it can scale, not just survive — the evidence a growth lender or the CEO would want before backing further expansion (Pack 18).
