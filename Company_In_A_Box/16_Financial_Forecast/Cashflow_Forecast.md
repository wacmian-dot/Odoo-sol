<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Cash — not profit — is what kills early ventures. The plan's cash logic is simple and robust: <b>a Start Up Loan provides the runway; the lean, gated cost base makes that runway last; recurring revenue turns cash-positive during Year 1–2 in the base case.</b> The free lead channel (Pack 08) is a major cash advantage — pipeline without upfront marketing spend.</div>

## Cash logic

| Element | Effect on cash |
|---|---|
| Start Up Loan (£25–100k, Pack 17) | Provides the opening runway |
| Lean cost base (founder-led Yr1) | Makes runway last |
| Free MCS lead channel | Pipeline with near-zero cash burn (Pack 08) |
| Recurring per-seat revenue | Compounds monthly; smooths cash |
| Traction-gated hiring/marketing | Cash out only follows cash in |

## The critical months

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Watch the trough</span><br>
The lowest cash point is the pre-revenue ramp of the first few months, before recurring revenue accumulates. The loan is sized to cover this trough with margin. Because acquisition (MCS channel) is nearly free and hiring is deferred, the trough is shallow by design — the plan's single most important cash-survival feature.</div>

## Sensitivity

Cash timing is most sensitive to how fast the first customers convert (Pack 08) and to loan size (Pack 17). Slower conversion deepens and lengthens the trough — mitigated by keeping costs low until revenue is proven.

## Implication

The cash plan is conservative and survivable: modest loan, shallow trough, recurring revenue, gated spend. This is exactly the profile a Start Up Loan is designed for and a cautious partner-CEO will respect.
