<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
A PESTEL scan shows the opportunity is powered mainly by <b>political/legal</b> and <b>environmental</b> forces that are durable but not permanent. The dominant risk is <b>policy reversal</b> (grant withdrawal, VAT change) — which is why the model must not assume the tailwind is guaranteed indefinitely (Pack 16 scenarios).</div>

## PESTEL scan

| Force | Reading | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Political** | Net-zero commitments; 600,000 heat pumps/yr by 2028 target; sustained cross-party direction of travel | ✅ Strong tailwind |
| **Economic** | Boiler Upgrade Scheme (£7,500); 0% VAT on solar to Mar 2027; cost-of-living pressure both drives energy-saving demand and constrains upfront spend | ⚠️ Mixed |
| **Social** | Rising consumer adoption of solar + heat pump "combos"; installer businesses professionalising | ✅ Tailwind |
| **Technological** | MCS digitalisation; growing expectation of software-run installers | ✅ Tailwind |
| **Environmental** | Statutory decarbonisation of heat and power — the structural driver of the entire installer boom | ✅ Strong, durable |
| **Legal** | MCS certification mandate; RECC/HIES consumer codes; UK GDPR obligations on any operator | ✅ Tailwind + compliance duty |

## The one to watch ⚠️

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Policy-reversal risk</span><br>
The most favourable forces are also the most reversible: grant schemes get cut, the 0% solar VAT has a March 2027 sunset, and targets can slip. A responsible plan treats the tailwind as <b>strong-but-conditional</b>. The financial model (Pack 16) therefore includes a bear case in which incentive support weakens, and the risk register (Pack 19) carries policy reversal as a named risk.</div>

## Implication

The external environment is genuinely favourable and, in its environmental/legal core, durable. But the venture should be built to survive a weaker-incentive world, not one that assumes the current subsidy regime forever. That discipline is what separates a credible plan from an optimistic one.
