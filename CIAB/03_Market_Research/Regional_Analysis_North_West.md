<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
A <b>Manchester / North West</b> launch is defensible on one verifiable anchor — Greater Manchester's 2038 carbon-neutral target, 12 years ahead of the national 2050 date — plus genuine regional trade density. It is a reasonable beachhead, but the choice should be treated as a starting hypothesis to validate against where the installer density and warm network actually are.</div>

## 1. The verifiable anchor ✅

Greater Manchester has a formally adopted target of **carbon neutrality by 2038** — a full 12 years ahead of the UK's 2050 target — confirmed directly by the Greater Manchester Combined Authority's Five-Year Environment Plan. Aggressive local decarbonisation correlates with concentrated demand for renewables installers, and therefore for the software they run.

## 2. Why a regional beachhead at all

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Focus beats spread</span><br>
A zero-base launch cannot cover the whole UK credibly. Concentrating early sales, references and trade-show presence in one high-density corridor (North West, extendable to the Midlands and Yorkshire) builds referenceable density faster than a thin national spread — the first UK case studies come from a cluster where word travels, not from scattered one-off wins.</div>

## 3. Rollout logic ⚠️

| Phase | Geography | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Beachhead | Greater Manchester / North West | Policy anchor + trade density; build first references |
| Expansion 1 | Midlands, Yorkshire | Adjacent high-density corridors; trade-show catchment (NEC Birmingham) |
| Expansion 2 | National | Once references and playbook are proven |

The specific ordering beyond the beachhead is a hypothesis (⚠️) to refine against real MCS-installer density data (Pack 05, Pack 06) rather than asserted as fixed.

## 4. Implication

Manchester is a sound, evidence-anchored place to start — and the two largest UK installer trade shows (Solar & Storage Live, InstallerSHOW) are both at the NEC in Birmingham, a short reach from a North West base (Pack 08). The launch geography and the lead-generation calendar reinforce each other.
