<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Track a <b>small set of leading and lagging indicators</b> — outreach volume, demos held, trials started, trial→paid, and revenue. Leading indicators (activity, demos) predict; lagging ones (revenue) confirm. Watch the conversion rates between stages most closely — they reveal where to fix.</div>

## The KPI set

| KPI | Type | Illustrative target (⚠️) |
|---|---|---|
| Outreaches / day | Leading | 40–60 (Pack 13) |
| Replies / week | Leading | ~30 |
| Demos held / week | Leading | 10–15 |
| Trials started / week | Leading | ~5 |
| Trial → paid | Lagging | 40%+ (⚠️ validate) |
| New customers / month | Lagging | 3 base (Pack 16) |
| MRR (your share) | Lagging | Grows with retained base |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Manage the ratios, not just the totals</span><br>
The gaps between stages (reply→demo, demo→trial, trial→paid) are where the money is made or lost. A 5-point gain in trial→paid beats a 20% rise in raw outreach. The dashboard's job is to make those ratios visible weekly so effort goes where leverage is highest (Pack 08).</div>

## Implication

This dashboard is the weekly operating rhythm (Pack 11) and the early-warning system for the financial model's assumptions (Pack 16). Targets are illustrative until real data replaces them.
