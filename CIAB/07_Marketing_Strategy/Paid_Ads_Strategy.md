<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Paid ads are a <b>last, CAC-gated</b> channel — switched on only after the message and funnel convert organically, and only if the cost-to-acquire clears a validated threshold. In a small, nameable market, paid is an amplifier of a proven motion, never the ignition.</div>

## When to turn paid on

| Precondition | Why |
|---|---|
| Outbound message proven to convert | Don't pay to amplify a message that doesn't work |
| Funnel conversion rates measured (Pack 08) | Needed to compute a real CAC |
| A validated CAC threshold (Pack 16) | Only spend where LTV/CAC clears the bar |

## Where paid could fit

- **LinkedIn** — precise targeting by job title (MD/ops lead) and industry; expensive but on-ICP.
- **Search** — bidding on the high-intent comparison terms (SEO Strategy) for immediate capture.

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Guardrail</span><br>
CAC in this niche is <b>unknown until measured</b> (Pack 16 Assumptions Register). Do not commit meaningful paid budget on assumed CAC. Run small, measured tests; scale only what demonstrably pays back within the target payback window (Pack 18 unit economics).</div>

## Implication

Paid ads sit at the bottom of the channel priority (Pack 07 Marketing Plan). Treating it as CAC-gated protects the loan-funded budget from the classic early-stage mistake of buying unprofitable growth.
