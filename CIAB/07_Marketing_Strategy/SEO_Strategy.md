<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
SEO is a <b>slow-burn, intent-capture</b> channel — worth a modest, focused investment on high-intent terms ("solar installer CRM UK", "heat pump job management software", "MCS compliant CRM"), not a large content-marketing programme. It compounds quietly while outbound does the immediate work.</div>

## Target intent clusters

| Cluster | Example terms |
|---|---|
| Vertical CRM | "solar installer CRM UK", "heat pump CRM", "renewables installer software" |
| Job/field management | "installer job management software UK", "field service software solar" |
| Compliance | "MCS compliant quoting software", "ECO4 / DNO paperwork software" |
| Comparison | "Payaca alternative", "Commusoft alternative for installers" |

## The approach

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Intent over volume</span><br>
Comparison and compliance terms are low-volume but <b>high-intent</b> — the searcher is already shopping. A handful of strong pages targeting these (plus the case studies from Content Strategy) will outperform broad, generic SEO for a niche B2B product. "Payaca alternative" is a particularly valuable term given the price-wedge positioning (Pack 04).</div>

## Boundary

SEO is a supporting channel, not the growth engine. Keep the investment proportionate; do not divert scarce early resource from outbound and onboarding, which drive near-term revenue.

## Implication

A small, intent-focused SEO footprint quietly lowers CAC over time and captures the self-directed buyers — a sensible, low-cost complement to the outbound motion.
