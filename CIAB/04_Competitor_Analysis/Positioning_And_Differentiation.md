<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
SalesOps CRM should claim one position: <b>"the proven, multi-trade installer platform priced for growing businesses — not just the ones that can already afford £1,000 a month."</b> It combines three verified advantages (proven scale, cross-vertical breadth, per-seat pricing) against the one gap both incumbents leave open: the small, growing installer.</div>

## 1. The positioning statement

> For growing UK renewables and multi-trade installers who have outgrown spreadsheets but aren't ready for £1,000-a-month flat-fee software, SalesOps CRM is the proven, multi-vertical installer platform — priced per seat so it scales with you, backed by 600+ live deployments.

## 2. The three pillars (each verified)

| Pillar | Evidence | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Proven** | 600+ deployments, 15,000+ users ✅ | Neutralises "unproven newcomer" |
| **Broad** | 8 verticals in one platform ✅ | Single-vertical / UK-only rivals |
| **Priced for growth** | Per-seat vs £999+/mo flat ✅ | Payaca's price ceiling; Reonic's enterprise tilt |

## 3. What we deliberately do NOT claim

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Discipline</span><br>
We do not claim superior MCS-feature depth, UK-native fluency, or specific integrations we have not demo-verified (Pack 20). We do not use the Polsat reference (Pack 02). Over-claiming against Payaca on its home turf, or leaning on an unverified client, would trade short-term punch for credibility — the opposite of the partner-grade standard this package holds to.</div>

## 4. Implication

This position is ownable because it is true and because neither incumbent occupies it. It flows directly into the pitch (Pack 09) and the ICP (Pack 06): sell proof and price to the growing small installer, hold breadth as the expansion story.
