<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Before a single deal, the likely win/loss pattern is predictable: we win small, growing, price-sensitive installers on price model and proof; we lose large or MCS-depth-obsessed buyers to Payaca and enterprise buyers to Reonic. These are hypotheses to validate and update with real pilot data (Pack 10).</div>

## Win / loss matrix (hypotheses ⚠️)

| Versus | We tend to WIN when… | We tend to LOSE when… |
|---|---|---|
| **The status quo** (spreadsheets + WhatsApp) | The installer is scaling and feeling the pain of dropped hand-offs, slow quotes, manual commission | They're too small/informal to need structure yet |
| **Payaca** | Buyer is small/cost-sensitive; values per-seat growth pricing; wants multi-vertical breadth | Buyer wants deepest UK-native MCS features and will pay the flat fee for them |
| **Reonic** | Buyer is a small/mid installer wanting fast, affordable adoption | Buyer is enterprise-scale wanting heavy project-design/simulation |

## The three recurring win themes

1. **"Proven, not a gamble."** 600+ deployments answers the newcomer objection every time.
2. **"Priced to start small."** Per-seat beats flat-fee for the growing installer.
3. **"One system for all your trades."** Breadth beats single-vertical tools for combo installers.

## The three recurring loss risks

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Manage these proactively</span><br>
(1) <b>UK-localisation doubt</b> — mitigated by demo-verifying MCS/DNO/ECO4 handling (Pack 20) and honest scoping (Pack 22 Q7). (2) <b>"You're new to the UK"</b> — mitigated by the proven-platform framing and fast first UK references (Pack 09). (3) <b>Demo reliability</b> — the demo instance was down once; never pitch without a confirmed working environment.</div>

## Implication

The sales motion (Pack 09) and pipeline stages (Pack 10) should be built to steer toward the win conditions (small, scaling, price-sensitive, multi-trade) and qualify out the loss conditions early, rather than chasing every deal.
