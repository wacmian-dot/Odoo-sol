<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Payaca is the strongest direct competitor: UK-native, renewables-specific, MCS-compliant, well-regarded. Its exposure is <b>price posture</b> — £999–1,199/month flat plus £1,500 onboarding — which prices out the smallest, fastest-growing installers. That segment is the entry wedge.</div>

## 1. Profile ✅

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Positioning | UK-native, dedicated platform for solar/heat-pump/battery/EV installers |
| Capability | CRM, pipeline, MCS-compliant proposals, scheduling, invoicing |
| Pricing | Growth £999–1,199/mo (annual vs monthly) + one-off £1,500 onboarding; Accelerator £299/mo for 12 months for new installers; SMS 4p extra; all ex-VAT |
| Reputation | Strong; often cited as the most complete UK renewables installer platform |

## 2. Strengths (respect them)

- **UK-native and MCS-fluent** — no localisation gap; speaks the compliance language natively.
- **Category leader mindshare** — often the default shortlist entry for UK renewables installers.
- **Complete workflow** — genuinely covers CRM→proposal→scheduling→invoicing.

## 3. Where it is beatable

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The price ceiling</span><br>
A ~£1,000/month + £1,500-upfront commitment is a real barrier for a 5–10-person installer just outgrowing spreadsheets — exactly the fastest-growing segment (Pack 06). A per-seat model (Pack 02) can enter that segment at a fraction of the cost. Payaca's own "Accelerator" discount to £299/mo for new installers is a tacit admission that its standard price deters the small end.</div>

- **Small-installer price sensitivity** — the wedge.
- **Single-market focus** — UK-only; RRUP's cross-vertical, multi-country platform depth is a different story (to be demo-verified).
- **Flat pricing** doesn't reward starting small and growing — per-seat does.

## 4. How NOT to compete

Do not attack Payaca on renewables-feature depth or MCS-fluency you haven't demo-verified — that is their home turf. Compete on **price model** and **breadth**, and on **the status quo** (Pack 09), not on out-Payaca-ing Payaca at its own game on day one.
