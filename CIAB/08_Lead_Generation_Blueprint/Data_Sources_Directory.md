<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The lead-source stack is anchored by two free, public, perfectly-matched registries (MCS + Companies House). Everything else is optional amplification. Used together with UK-GDPR discipline (Pack 15), they produce a compliant, high-fit prospect base at minimal cost.</div>

## Directory

| Source | Yields | Cost | Compliance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MCS Installation Database** | Every certified UK installer — the ICP universe | Free | Public; check terms before automated scraping |
| **Companies House SIC search** | Firm size, status, filings for size-filtering | Free | Public |
| **LinkedIn** | Named MD / ops-lead per firm | Free / Sales Nav | Respect platform terms; UK GDPR on contact use |
| **RECC / HIES directories** | Certified installer membership; partnership route | Free | Public bodies |
| **Paid B2B data** (B2B Data Scout, Prospect360, More Than Words) | Verified emails/phones at scale | Paid (get quotes) | Ensure GDPR-compliant sourcing + lawful basis |
| **RRUP EU customer base** | Warm referrals with UK links | — | ❓ Ask RRUP |

## Using them lawfully

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">UK GDPR is not optional</span><br>
B2B outreach in the UK still requires a lawful basis (typically legitimate interest for B2B email, with a clear opt-out), ICO registration, and suppression of anyone who opts out. Never import a third party's proprietary client list (e.g. from a prior employer). Pack 15 covers the policies; this is a live compliance obligation from the first email.</div>

## Implication

The two free registries alone are enough to run month-one outreach. Paid data is a scale accelerant to add once the message is proven to convert — not a prerequisite.
