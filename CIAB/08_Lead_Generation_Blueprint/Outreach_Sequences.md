<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Run a disciplined multi-touch cadence (email + call + LinkedIn) over ~2–3 weeks per prospect, anchored on the "leaky bucket" message — lead with the <b>cost of the status quo</b>, not features. Specific numbers in the copy are illustrative placeholders to replace with real discovery findings, never invented statistics.</div>

## 1. The cadence (per prospect)

| Day | Touch | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email 1 (leaky-bucket hook) | Introduce the pain, not the product |
| 3 | LinkedIn connect + light note | Warm the name |
| 5 | Call 1 | Conversation; book a demo |
| 8 | Email 2 (proof: 600+ deployments, per-seat) | Address "who are you" + price |
| 12 | Call 2 | Second attempt; different angle |
| 15 | Email 3 (break-up / free-trial offer) | Low-pressure door-opener |

## 2. The message spine — "leaky bucket"

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Lead with cost of inaction</span><br>
"We work with growing UK installers whose teams lose deals because quotes take too long, and whose margins leak through manual paperwork and commission maths. We've built one system that runs the whole job — lead to signed contract to commission — and it's priced per seat, so it fits a growing team, not just a big one. 600+ installers already run it. Worth a 20-minute look?"</div>

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Honesty rule on numbers</span><br>
Any specific figure in outreach (e.g. "installers lose X% of deals") must come from real discovery or published data — never a fabricated statistic. Use ranges and questions ("how long does a quote take you today?") until you have your own proof points.</div>

## 3. What each touch must and must not do

- **Must:** be specific to the installer trade; offer the free 30-day trial; make the next step tiny (a 20-min demo).
- **Must not:** reference Polsat or any unverified client (Pack 02); quote a fixed UK price (Pack 22 Q); over-claim features not demo-verified.

## Implication

This cadence is the SDR's daily engine (Pack 10, Pack 11). It is designed to convert the free MCS list into booked demos — the single metric that most predicts revenue in the first quarter.
