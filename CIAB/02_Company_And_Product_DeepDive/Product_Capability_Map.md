<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
SalesOps CRM is not a generic CRM — it is an <b>end-to-end operating system for installation trades</b>, covering lead → quote → contract → job → invoice → commission in one system. Its differentiated core is the <b>Work-Flow engine</b> and its cross-vertical breadth. Feature specifics that depend on the live demo are flagged; everything else is verified from the public product.</div>

## 1. The organising idea: one system, whole lifecycle ✅

The product's central claim is unified lifecycle management for physical-installation businesses. Where a typical installer stitches together a basic CRM, spreadsheets for quoting, WhatsApp for crew coordination, and manual compliance paperwork, SalesOps CRM collapses that into one workspace.

| Lifecycle stage | Capability ✅ |
|---|---|
| Lead capture | Automated sales funnel, no manual hand-offs between stages |
| Quote / proposal | Branded PDF quotes on the client's own letterhead |
| Contract | Contract generation + e-signature with contractors |
| Job / installation | Work-Flow engine: task automation, status routing, crew assignment |
| Completion | Completion protocols / documents on client letterhead |
| Invoice / commission | Auto commission calculation on status completion |
| Oversight | Reporting/analytics: pipeline, user activity, install/job reports |

## 2. The differentiated core — the Work-Flow engine ✅

The Work-Flow engine is the capability most worth leading with. It lets a business define automated task chains tied to contract stages: auto-assign tasks, fire SMS/system notifications at each step, route responsibility to named people or teams, and **automatically calculate sales commission** when a defined status is reached. For a growing installer, manual commission calculation and dropped hand-offs are two of the most acute operational pains — and this addresses both directly.

## 3. Extensibility ✅

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Why this matters for the UK</span><br>
100+ pre-built add-on modules plus an open API mean UK-specific requirements (MCS/DNO/ECO4 compliance fields, UK VAT, GBP invoicing) are a <b>configuration and extension exercise on a proven platform</b>, not a rebuild. This is a materially faster and lower-risk localisation path than a from-scratch product — subject to confirming with RRUP exactly what UK localisation already exists (Pack 22, Q7).</div>
