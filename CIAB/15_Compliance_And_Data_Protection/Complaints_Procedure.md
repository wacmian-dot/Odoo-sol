<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
A short, fair complaints procedure signals professionalism to installers (a trust-driven trade) and catches problems before they become churn or reputation damage. Acknowledge fast, resolve fairly, learn from it.</div>

## The procedure

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1. Acknowledge | Respond within one business day; the customer knows they're heard |
| 2. Understand | Get the full picture; don't defend reflexively |
| 3. Resolve | Fix it fairly and promptly; involve RRUP if it's a product issue (Pack 14) |
| 4. Follow up | Confirm the customer is satisfied |
| 5. Learn | Log the complaint; feed patterns into product/process improvement |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Complaints are retention opportunities</span><br>
In a tight-knit trade, how you handle a problem is remembered and talked about more than the problem itself. A well-handled complaint often produces a more loyal customer — and a referral — than one who never had an issue. Treat it as a retention and reputation tool, not a nuisance.</div>

## Implication

A basic complaints process is low-effort and high-value in a word-of-mouth market (Pack 07). It also surfaces product issues to raise with RRUP early.
