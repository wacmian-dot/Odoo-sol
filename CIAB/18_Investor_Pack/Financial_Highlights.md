## What the numbers say

- **Capital-light:** launch on a Start Up Loan, not a large raise.
- **Recurring & compounding:** per-seat SaaS revenue accumulates with retention.
- **Wide range:** early-stage uncertainty is real — hence the scenario view, not a single number.

## The honesty line

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Status of these numbers</span><br>
These are illustrative scenario outputs driven by explicit assumptions, two of which (UK price, revenue share) are unset pending the CEO. They show the <b>business can work across a plausible range</b> — which is the honest claim to make pre-pilot, and a stronger one than a false-precision forecast.</div>

## Implication

The highlights support a modest loan now and an equity conversation later, once pilot data replaces the assumptions.
