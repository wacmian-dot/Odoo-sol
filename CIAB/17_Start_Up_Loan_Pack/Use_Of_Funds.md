<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The loan funds the <b>cash trough</b>, not a spending spree: founder runway through the pre-revenue ramp, essential legal/formation, and a modest first marketing presence — with the largest discretionary items (hiring, big trade stands) deferred until traction justifies them.</div>

## Illustrative allocation

| Use | Share (⚠️) | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Founder runway (pre-revenue months) | ~40% | Cover the cash trough (Pack 16) |
| Legal / formation / compliance | ~15% | Proper agreement + ICO/DPA (Pack 14/15) — non-negotiable |
| Marketing (1 trade show + web/content) | ~20% | Proportionate presence (Pack 07) |
| Localisation / setup (if needed) | ~15% | Subject to RRUP scope (Q7) |
| Contingency | ~10% | Prudence |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The discipline a lender wants to see</span><br>
Note what's <b>not</b> funded upfront: no full sales team, no big paid-ads budget, no large stands. Those come from traction (Pack 05 growth gates), not from the loan. That gated discipline is exactly what makes the repayment path credible.</div>

## Implication

A lender (and the CEO) sees capital allocated to survival and proof, not speculation — the profile of a fundable, low-risk plan.
