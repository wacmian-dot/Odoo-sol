## Sensitivity — what moves the needle

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The two dominant levers</span><br>
(1) <b>Trial→paid conversion</b> — a few points here swings revenue more than any amount of extra outreach, which is why onboarding quality (Pack 12) is the top operational investment. (2) <b>UK price per seat</b> — sets both the wedge and the margin; the single most important CEO/RRUP decision for the model. Everything else (churn, seat count) matters less within plausible ranges.</div>

## Breakeven & runway

- Breakeven customer count depends on the (gated) revenue share and cost base — illustratively low-tens of customers in the base case, achievable within Year 1–2 at 3/month.
- A Start Up Loan (£25–100k, Pack 17) provides a runway consistent with reaching base-case breakeven if conversion holds — which is why the loan, not heavy equity, fits.

## Reconciliation note

This scenario set is reconciled against the GTM viability simulation (Pack _SIMULATION) — the funnel (Pack 08) and staffing capacity (Pack 13) must be internally consistent with these revenue figures. Any contradiction is resolved there, not averaged.

## Implication

The business is viable across a realistic range provided the base-case conversion is achievable and pricing preserves the wedge. It is not viable as a high-burn play — the analysis argues firmly for lean, gated spending.
