## Dependency

Year 3 assumes Years 1–2 validated the motion and retention held. It is a destination the plan aims at, not a promise — and its value is showing the ceiling is worth the climb.

## Implication

The three-year arc — prove, expand, scale — is coherent and, in the base case, reaches a genuinely valuable position. The bear case (Pack Scenario) still survives; the bull case funds faster expansion.
