<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Year 1 is a <b>proof year, not a profit year</b>: build the beachhead, keep the base lean (largely founder-led), reach ~30 customers under base assumptions, and cover the modest cost base without heavy hiring. The plan is designed so Year 1 is survivable on a Start Up Loan even if revenue runs to the bear case.</div>

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The Year-1 discipline</span><br>
Year 1 deliberately does not hire the full pod or take big stands. It proves the motion with a lean base so that Year 2 expansion is funded by demonstrated traction, not hope. This is exactly what a Start Up Loan mentor and a partner-CEO want to see: spend gated on proof.</div>

## Dependencies

Revenue share (Q1/Q9) and UK price change these figures materially. The <i>shape</i> — lean, proof-focused, loan-covered — holds regardless.

## Implication

Year 1's job is evidence: 3+ references, a repeatable motion, and validated conversion. Hit that, and Year 2 (next docs) becomes an expansion funded by traction.
