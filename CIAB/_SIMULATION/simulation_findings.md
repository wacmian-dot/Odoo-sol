# Simulation & Red-Team — Working Findings
**Internal working file (not a partner PDF).** Records the four simulations from the plan's Part 5A and where their findings were folded into the Packs. Flags: ✅/📊/⚠️/❓/🚩 as elsewhere.

## 1. Financial sensitivity & scenario simulation
- Varied Assumptions-Register drivers (UK £/seat £12–25; trial→paid; churn; revenue share) across bear/base/bull.
- **Finding:** base and bull clear breakeven within a sensible horizon; bear survives on the lean cost base and long loan term. The two dominant levers are **trial→paid conversion** and **UK price per seat**.
- **Folded into:** Pack 16 Scenario_And_Sensitivity (scenario fan, dominant-lever callout), Pack 17 (repayment serviceable from bear case).
- **Flag:** all outputs ⚠️ illustrative until UK price/revenue share set (❓ CEO).

## 2. Competitive war-game
- Simulated Payaca and Reonic responses to a SalesOps UK entry.
- **Payaca likely response:** lean on UK-native/MCS depth and brand; possibly extend "Accelerator" discounting to defend the small end. **Our counter:** per-seat wedge + breadth + proof; don't fight on MCS depth.
- **Reonic likely response:** continue enterprise-tilt UK push with capital. **Our counter:** own the small-installer segment they underserve; compete on focus/speed not spend.
- **Folded into:** Pack 04 (Win_Loss_Themes, Payaca/Reonic deep-dives), Pack 09 (Objection_Handling).

## 3. Adversarial CEO/CIO red-team
- Role-played (a) a skeptical RRUP CEO and (b) a skeptical installer buyer; generated hardest questions.
- **Hardest questions surfaced:** Which deal structure are we actually in? Can you substantiate Polsat? What UK localisation exists? Why would an installer trust a brand-new UK operation? How is CAC/churn actually known?
- **Result:** each maps to an existing answer or an explicit ❓ flag — none left unaddressed. The Polsat and deal-structure items are correctly the top two CEO questions.
- **Folded into:** Pack 22 (Ten_Questions ordering), Pack 09 (Objection_Handling), Pack 02 (Polsat flag).

## 4. Go-to-market viability simulation
- Cross-checked funnel (Pack 08: ~1,000 outreaches → ~12 paid) against staffing capacity (Pack 13: 1 SDR at 40–60/day) and the revenue model (Pack 16: ~3 customers/month base).
- **Finding:** internally consistent — one focused SDR can generate the outreach volume implied by the base-case customer additions; no contradiction between funnel, capacity and revenue.
- **Folded into:** Pack 08 Conversion_Plan (reconciliation note), Pack 16 (reconciliation note), Pack 13 capacity model.

## Net
No simulation surfaced a fatal contradiction. The two genuinely unresolved items are the **deal structure** and **UK pricing** (both ❓ CEO) — correctly reflected as gating everything financial. The plan is internally consistent across market, funnel, staffing and finance at base assumptions.
