<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The venture's top risks are known and mostly controllable: <b>deal-structure ambiguity</b>, an <b>unverified client claim</b>, <b>well-funded competition</b>, and the classic <b>50/50 deadlock</b>. None is a reason not to proceed; each has a named mitigation and a clear owner. Surfacing them proactively is itself a mark of the plan's seriousness.</div>
