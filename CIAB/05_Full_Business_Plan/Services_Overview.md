<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The UK operation sells a <b>per-seat SaaS subscription to the SalesOps CRM platform</b>, wrapped in a high-touch onboarding service that is itself a differentiator — proven software, delivered with founder-level implementation care.</div>

## The offering

| Component | What it is |
|---|---|
| Core subscription | Per-seat access to SalesOps CRM (lead→quote→contract→job→invoice→commission) |
| Vertical configuration | Trade-specific setup (solar / heat pump / multi-trade) |
| Onboarding service | 30-day hands-on implementation (data migration, config, training) — Pack 12 |
| Ongoing support | Retention support; expansion as the customer adds seats |

## The delivery model

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Service is the moat</span><br>
The software is proven but not unique in every feature. The <b>delivery experience</b> — a dedicated implementation lead who migrates data and gets the team live inside the free trial — is where a lean, founder-led UK operation can genuinely out-care larger, more automated competitors. Sell the software; win on the onboarding.</div>

## What is in / out of scope

- **In:** subscription, configuration, onboarding, support, expansion.
- **Out (initially):** bespoke product development (that sits with RRUP), deep custom ERP integrations (scoped case-by-case, Pack 20).

## Implication

The onboarding service (Pack 12) is not overhead — it is the differentiated part of the offering and the primary driver of trial→paid conversion. Resource it accordingly (Pack 13).
