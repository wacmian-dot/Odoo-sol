<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The plan: localise a proven Polish installer-CRM into the UK via a lean, founder-led operation, winning the underserved small-installer segment on proof and per-seat pricing, funded initially by a UK Start Up Loan. The opportunity is real and the product is proven; the two open variables are the <b>deal structure</b> and <b>UK pricing</b>, both CEO-gated.</div>

## 1. Business model (canvas, condensed)

| Block | This venture |
|---|---|
| Value proposition | Proven, multi-trade installer platform, priced per seat for growing firms |
| Customer segments | MCS solar/heat-pump installers, 10–50 staff (Pack 06) |
| Channels | Free MCS-database outreach; trade shows; RECC/HIES (Pack 08) |
| Revenue | Per-seat SaaS (UK price ❓); split with RRUP per deal structure (❓) |
| Key resources | RRUP platform + brand; the lean UK pod (Pack 13) |
| Key partners | RRUP (parent/supplier); consumer-code bodies |
| Cost structure | Lean pod salaries, marketing, legal, localisation (Pack 16) |
| Key activities | Sell, onboard, retain; localise |

## 2. SWOT

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| Proven platform (600+ deployments); per-seat price wedge; cross-vertical breadth | No UK presence yet; unverified UK feature depth; deal structure unresolved |
| **Opportunities** | **Threats** |
| Fast-growing, policy-backed market; underserved small-installer segment | Payaca (incumbent), Reonic (funded entrant); policy reversal; capital asymmetry |

## 3. Growth strategy (Ansoff)

Start in **market penetration** (existing product, target UK segment). Then **market development** (adjacent trades, regions — Pack 06 Tier 2). Hold **product development** (UK-specific features) as a localisation workstream with RRUP, not a from-scratch build.

## 4. The two gating variables ❓

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">What must be resolved</span><br>
(1) <b>Deal structure</b> — equity JV vs commission/agency (Pack 14, Pack 22 Q1) — determines liability, funding and economics. (2) <b>UK pricing</b> — sets the wedge and the model (Pack 02, Pack 16). The plan is robust to either resolution, but cannot be finalised until both are answered by the CEO.</div>

## 5. Why it can work

A proven product + a real, growing, underserved segment + a lean, low-burn entry + a free-trial motion that de-risks the buyer = a credible, fundable venture. Reonic's VC-backed entry into the same space independently validates the thesis (Pack 04).
