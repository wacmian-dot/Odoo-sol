<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Grow in three evidence-gated phases: <b>prove</b> (win a Tier-1 cluster with a lean pod), <b>expand</b> (adjacent segments/regions once repeatable), <b>scale</b> (larger deals via the SAP play, and a growing team). Each phase is triggered by proof, not calendar — which keeps burn matched to traction.</div>

## The three phases

| Phase | Focus | Trigger to advance |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Prove** (~0–6 mo) | Beachhead: Tier-1 installers, North West; lean pod; free-trial motion | 3+ referenceable customers + repeatable demo→paid |
| **2 — Expand** (~6–18 mo) | Adjacent trades/regions (Pack 06 Tier 2); trade-show presence; first hires beyond the pod | Positive unit economics (Pack 16); playbook documented |
| **3 — Scale** (18 mo+) | Tier-3 SAP-coexistence deals; regional team; national reach | Proven larger-deal motion; funding for growth |

## The discipline

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Traction gates spend</span><br>
Do not hire the full team, take the big trade-show stands, or chase enterprise deals until Phase 1 proof exists. Matching investment to demonstrated traction is what keeps a loan-funded venture solvent and what a Start Up Loan mentor (Pack 17) will expect to see.</div>

## Dependencies ⚠️

Phase pacing depends on the deal structure (Pack 14) and funding (Pack 16/17), both partly CEO-gated. The <i>shape</i> of the path is robust; the <i>speed</i> depends on those answers.

## Implication

This staged path is the backbone of the launch plan (Pack 21) and the financial model's timeline (Pack 16). It is deliberately conservative on spend and aggressive on focus.
