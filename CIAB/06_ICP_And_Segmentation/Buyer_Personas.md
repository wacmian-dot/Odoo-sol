<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
In a 10–50-person installer, two people decide: the <b>owner/MD</b> (buys growth, control and margin) and the <b>operations lead</b> (buys relief from admin chaos). Speak to both — the MD signs, but the ops lead's daily pain is what makes the case urgent.</div>

## Persona 1 — The Owner / Managing Director

| | |
|---|---|
| Wants | Growth without losing control; visible pipeline; protected margin; less chaos as they scale |
| Fears | Paying £1,000+/mo for shelfware; a painful switch that disrupts a busy install schedule; being locked in |
| Responds to | "Proven — 600+ deployments"; "priced to start small and grow"; the cost of the status quo in £ |
| Decides on | Commercial case + confidence it will actually get adopted |

## Persona 2 — The Operations Lead

| | |
|---|---|
| Wants | Fewer dropped jobs; faster quotes; no more chasing crews on WhatsApp; automatic commission maths |
| Fears | Another system nobody uses; messy data migration; more admin, not less |
| Responds to | The Work-Flow engine demo; the 30-day onboarding with a dedicated "personal tutor" (Pack 12) |
| Decides on | Will this actually make my week easier by day 30? |

## The combined play

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Sell the pain to ops, the case to the MD</span><br>
Discovery (Pack 09) should surface the ops lead's daily pain quantitatively (hours lost, deals slipped), then present the MD with that pain costed in pounds and the per-seat, proven, low-risk answer. The free 30-day trial with hands-on onboarding is what closes the ops lead's adoption fear — the single biggest silent deal-killer.</div>

## Implication

Outreach (Pack 08) targets both titles; the demo (Pack 09) is structured to give each persona their moment. These personas are hypotheses to sharpen with real discovery calls — update them as pilot data arrives.
