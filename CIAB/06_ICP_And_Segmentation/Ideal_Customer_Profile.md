<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The first-beachhead ICP is a <b>MCS-certified solar and/or heat-pump installer with roughly 10–50 employees</b> — large enough to feel the pain of spreadsheets and WhatsApp, small enough to move fast and to be priced out by Payaca's flat fee. This is the segment where all three of our advantages (proof, breadth, per-seat price) land hardest.</div>

## 1. The ICP, precisely

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Certification | MCS-certified (in the public database — reachable) ✅ |
| Trade | Solar PV and/or heat pumps (the two largest, fastest-growing UK segments) |
| Size | ~10–50 employees / a handful to a dozen+ software seats |
| Stage | Scaling — outgrowing spreadsheets/WhatsApp, not yet locked into a multi-year incumbent contract |
| Geography | North West beachhead first (Pack 03), extensible to Midlands/Yorkshire |
| Pain | Slow quotes, dropped hand-offs, manual compliance paperwork, manual commission calculation |

## 2. Why this segment (the scoring logic)

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Where our wedge is sharpest</span><br>
This segment scores highest on a simple weighting of <b>reachability</b> (every firm is in the MCS database), <b>pain intensity</b> (10–50 staff is exactly where informal tools break), <b>switching readiness</b> (no entrenched incumbent contract), and <b>price fit</b> (per-seat undercuts Payaca's £999+/mo). No other segment scores as well on all four at once.</div>

## 3. Who to deliberately disqualify

| Not now | Why |
|---|---|
| Sub-5-person owner-operators | Too informal/price-sensitive to adopt; low ARR, high support burden |
| Enterprise installers on Salesforce/SAP | Long cycles, heavy customisation; wrong first battle |
| Firms mid-contract with Payaca/Commusoft | Switching cost too high right now — revisit at renewal |

Disqualifying well is as valuable as targeting well: it keeps the lean pod (Pack 13) focused on winnable deals.

## 4. Implication

Every channel (Pack 08), sequence (Pack 08), demo (Pack 09) and metric (Pack 10) is tuned to this ICP. When in doubt about a prospect, the test is: *scaling MCS solar/heat-pump installer, 10–50 staff, not locked in?* If not, deprioritise.
