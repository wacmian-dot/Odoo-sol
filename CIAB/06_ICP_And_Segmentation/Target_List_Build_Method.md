<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The target list is built for near-zero cost from the <b>public MCS Installation Database</b>, cross-referenced with Companies House (size) and LinkedIn (named decision-makers). This turns "who do I even call" into a ranked list of named MDs and ops leads at qualifying installers — the single highest-leverage week-one task.</div>

## The method, step by step

1. **Pull qualifying installers** from the MCS Installation Database — filter to solar PV and heat pump certifications in the beachhead region. ✅ (public)
2. **Size-filter via Companies House** — SIC-code + employee/turnover signals to keep firms in the ~10–50 staff band. ✅ (free, public)
3. **Name the decision-makers** — cross-reference each firm on LinkedIn to identify the Owner/MD and Operations Lead (the two personas). ⚠️ (manual/semi-automated)
4. **Score and rank** — apply the ICP scoring (reachability, pain, switching-readiness, price-fit) to prioritise.
5. **Enrich contactability** — add verified emails/phones (from the firm's own site or a paid B2B data source such as B2B Data Scout / Prospect360 where needed). 📊

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">Compliance note</span><br>
Check the MCS database's terms of use before any automated scraping, and handle all contact data under UK GDPR (Pack 15) — lawful basis, suppression of opt-outs, and an ICO registration in place. Do not import any third party's proprietary client list.</div>

## Output

A living, ranked spreadsheet: firm · trade · size · MD name · ops-lead name · contact · ICP score · status. This is the SDR's daily worklist and the top of the pipeline (Pack 10).

## Implication

Because the core source is free and perfectly matched to the ICP, month-one pipeline does not wait on budget or trade shows — the SDR can begin qualified outreach on day one (Pack 08).
