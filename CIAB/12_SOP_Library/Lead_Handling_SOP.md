<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Every lead follows the same path: qualify against ICP → log → begin the cadence → book discovery. Consistency here is what makes the funnel math (Pack 08) real rather than aspirational.</div>

## Procedure

1. **Receive/identify lead** (MCS list, inbound, referral, trade show).
2. **Qualify against ICP** (Pack 06): MCS solar/HP installer, 10–50 staff, not locked in. If not, tag "nurture/disqualify" with reason.
3. **Log in CRM** at Stage 1 with source tag (Pack 10).
4. **Start the outreach cadence** (Pack 08 sequence).
5. **On reply**, advance to "Contacted"; aim to book a 20-min discovery/demo.
6. **If no reply after the full cadence**, move to nurture; re-touch in 60–90 days.

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">The one non-negotiable</span><br>
Log every lead and every outcome. An un-logged lead is a lost lead and a blind spot in the KPIs. The CRM is the single source of truth for the pipeline — if it isn't in there, it didn't happen.</div>

## Implication

This SOP feeds Stage 1→3 of the pipeline (Pack 10) and keeps top-of-funnel disciplined. It is the first SOP a new SDR (Pack 13) is trained on.
