<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Run a <b>full-cycle, trial-led motion</b>: qualified outreach → 20-minute vertical demo → free 30-day trial with hands-on onboarding → paid conversion. The free trial is the centre of gravity — it converts "unproven newcomer" into "already running in your business" before money is discussed.</div>

## The motion, stage by stage

| Stage | Goal | Owner | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Prospect | Booked, qualified demo | SDR | ICP-fit prospect agrees to 20-min demo |
| 2. Discover | Surface + quantify pain | AE | 2–3 concrete pains costed (leaky bucket) |
| 3. Demo | Show the pain solved, live | AE | Prospect sees their own workflow run |
| 4. Trial | 30-day hands-on trial | AE + Implementation | Data migrated, team using it by day 7 |
| 5. Convert | Trial → paid | AE | Signed contract via e-signature |

## Why trial-led

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Proof beats persuasion</span><br>
For a newcomer to the UK, nothing de-risks the buy like the prospect running the software in their own business for free for 30 days. It flips the burden of proof, neutralises the "you're new here" objection, and lets the product's genuine strengths (Work-Flow engine, breadth) do the selling. The onboarding process (Pack 12) is what makes the trial convert rather than fizzle.</div>

## The lean-team adaptation

With a small pod (Pack 13), the AE runs discovery→demo→close as one relationship (no SDR/AE hand-off friction), while a dedicated Implementation lead owns the trial's success. This keeps trust with one commercial contact and protects conversion.

## Implication

Every metric (Pack 10), SOP (Pack 12) and staffing choice (Pack 13) serves this motion. The two highest-leverage skills to hire and train for are **running a sharp vertical demo** and **driving a trial to adoption by day 7**.
