<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Given the prior partner's misrepresentations, <b>nothing should be verbal</b>. This checklist enumerates the terms to confirm in writing under either structure, so the reset with the CEO produces a clean, documented baseline — not another round of assumptions.</div>

## Terms to confirm — both structures

| Term | Confirm |
|---|---|
| Contributions | What RRUP provides (product, brand, IP licence, capital) vs. what you provide (Pack 22 Q3) |
| Revenue mechanics | Commission % or equity split; on TCV or recurring; renewals included? (Q9) |
| Territory | UK exclusivity — granted? conditions? what revokes it? (Q8) |
| IP & brand | Who owns UK customer relationships, data, the SalesOps CRM UK brand |
| Term & exit | Duration, notice, what happens to customers/commission on exit |
| Liability | Your exposure for product faults, data, engineering |
| Support obligations | What RRUP must provide (product, localisation, technical) (Q7) |

## Equity-JV-specific terms

Reserved matters; board composition; deadlock mechanism; capital calls; valuation method; drag/tag rights (see Deadlock_And_Exit).

## Agency-specific terms

Commission schedule and timing; clawback conditions; minimum performance; post-termination commission tail; confirmation of no product/infra liability.

<div class="warn"><span class="lbl">The discipline</span><br>
Every item above goes into a written agreement drafted/reviewed by a UK solicitor (see Solicitor_Brief) before signing. The cost of proper drafting is trivial against the cost of a misunderstanding — a lesson the Bart episode already taught at no small price.</div>

## Implication

Take this checklist into the CEO conversation and into the solicitor engagement. It turns "we broadly agreed" into a documented, enforceable baseline.
