<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
The daily rhythm is simple and outreach-led: <b>prospect in the morning, demos and trials in the afternoon, admin last.</b> Protect selling time; let automation handle reminders. Consistency of daily activity is what produces the funnel volumes the model depends on.</div>

## The daily rhythm

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| Morning | Outreach block — new touches + follow-ups (Pack 08 cadence); target 40–60 touches |
| Midday | Demos held (Pack 09) |
| Afternoon | Trial onboarding tasks (Pack 12); discovery calls |
| Late | Log activity in CRM; review tomorrow's demos; respond to inbound |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Protect the outreach block</span><br>
The single most important daily discipline is a protected morning outreach block. It's the top of the funnel; if it slips, everything downstream dries up 3–4 weeks later. Everything else can flex around it — this cannot.</div>

## Implication

A consistent daily rhythm is what turns the plan's assumptions into reality. The weekly and monthly cadences (this Pack) roll up from these daily habits.
