<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Monthly, zoom out: reconcile actuals against the financial model, update the Assumptions Register with real data, review progress against the growth-phase gates, and keep the RRUP relationship actively managed. This is where the plan gets corrected, not just executed.</div>

## Monthly agenda

| Item | Action |
|---|---|
| Financial actuals | Compare to Pack 16; update Assumptions Register with real conversion/CAC/churn |
| Growth-gate check | Are we meeting the Phase-1 triggers? (Pack 05) |
| Risk review | Update the risk register (Pack 19) with what's changed |
| RRUP check-in | Product, support, localisation, any open terms (Pack 14) |
| Reference building | Secured a new UK case study this month? (Pack 07/09) |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Feed real data back into the model</span><br>
The single highest-value monthly task is replacing an Assumptions-Register estimate with a real number. Each month the financial model gets more real and the plan gets sharper — the model is a living instrument, not a one-time forecast.</div>

## Implication

The monthly rhythm is what turns the illustrative financial model (Pack 16) into a validated one over time, and keeps the RRUP partnership healthy — the two things most likely to make or break the venture.
