<div class="bluf"><span class="lbl">Bottom line up front</span><br>
Even at a team of one, name the <b>functions</b> explicitly — sell, onboard, run — so that when you hire (Pack 13), responsibilities transfer cleanly rather than being reconstructed. Clear ownership prevents the "everything is mine and nothing is done" trap of an early founder-run operation.</div>

## Function ownership (scales with the team)

| Function | Owner at launch | Owner once pod hired (Pack 13) |
|---|---|---|
| Prospecting / outreach | You | SDR |
| Discovery / demo / close | You | Vertical AE |
| Trial onboarding / retention | You | Implementation lead |
| Strategy / P&L / RRUP relationship | You | You (MD) |
| Compliance / admin | You | You / outsourced |

<div class="callout"><span class="lbl">Design the org before you need it</span><br>
Naming functions now means each hire steps into a defined role with documented SOPs (Pack 12), not a vague "help out." That's what lets a lean team scale without chaos — and what a partner-CEO wants to see you've thought through.</div>

## Implication

This mapping is the bridge from the daily/weekly/monthly rhythms (this Pack) to the staffing plan (Pack 13). It keeps ownership clear at every team size.
